Players

One headset, or two in the same room: one hosts (holds the mallet and keeps the batch's time), the other joins and kneads with their hands.

Settings

Dough quality: finer dough looks and feels better but costs more; on a Quest the richer levels run in slow motion.

How to play

Steamed rice drops into the mortar and is pounded into mochi: one person swings the heavy mallet; between strikes, someone kneads and folds the dough with their own hands. Choose where the mortar stands under Worlds.
